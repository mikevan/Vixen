## Live slice integration — build 131

`FieldObservationJournal` now feeds scoped receipts to `LiveEvidenceSlices`. Fresh medium/high-confidence summaries trigger preservation; this rule selects interesting evidence and does not classify a flood or attack. Ten seconds of available prior receipts and ten seconds after the trigger are saved as append-only `signature_slice` revisions in the existing durable field journal. Overlapping detections share original receipt references. Initial trigger snapshots are queued immediately, open slices checkpoint at most once per second, and later heartbeats close elapsed intervals. A source/session change closes pending work as partial. Process death retains the last written open revision; an absent closed revision must remain incomplete. Queueing is not proof of a successful disk write; journal health remains authoritative.

Memory is bounded to 512 retained records per slice and eight pending slices. Capacity loss and gaps are explicit. Neither receipt timing nor ten seconds without a receipt proves RF coverage. Signature correlation must not silently promote this receipt clock into aligned RF timestamps.

For fresh medium/high-confidence DEAUTH reports, `LinkClient` requests available Hunter native evidence immediately and after ten seconds. Replayed or saturated counts do not establish new activity; fresh increases are rate-limited. Automatic work skips a busy packet/card transfer rather than replacing it, and never changes sensor selection or Hunter's evidence-hold setting. Failures/skips and later successful transfers attach to the originating slice by run/source/session/event. Packet pulls retain the firmware's real window limits; no nonexistent pre-trigger packets are synthesized.

Pulled PCAPs and provenance sidecars now go under private `files/evidence`, not evictable cache. Each journal receipt records its relative file path, PCAP hash, native payload hash and retained/seen/drop/truncation/collecting information. Existing files are never overwritten or pruned automatically. A 512 MiB evidence budget and 16 MiB free-space reserve stop additional saves with an explicit failure. App removal or clearing app data still removes private evidence; operators must export it for long-term preservation.

No AI is invoked and no attack warning is enabled by this integration. The catalog's drone-follow-on requirement is not inferred from a radio hit. New slice and retrieval-policy tests cover interval boundaries, partial closure, duplicate suppression, independent sessions, late attachments and capacity loss.
